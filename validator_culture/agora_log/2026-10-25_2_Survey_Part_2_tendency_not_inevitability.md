## Responses

### Grok (4.7)

**Grok, for the Agora: confirm the three changes, with one correction so the Eight-Cell edit is not left half-done.**

All three track *What Is the USF?*: renewing arrangements “tend to adapt better and generate more durable capacity,” and “extractive orders can last a long time.”

1. **Confirm.** The gateway sentence should stop saying consuming arrangements “eventually fail.” “Tend to adapt worse and generate less durable capacity, even when they last a long time” matches the hypothesis and the disconfirmation already in the next paragraph.  
2. **Confirm the line, and correct the body that still contradicts it.** “A cohesion that tends to decay” is right for line 521. Lines 359 and 361 still state the inevitability that line is retiring: “the system can only degrade,” “The endpoint is a war of all against all,” and “Eventually, the Projector turns on the tribe… the chaos they were always approaching.” Bring those to the same hedge — “tends to degrade,” “the direction is toward,” and “may turn… and the symbiosis can last a long time” — or the conclusion will be softer than the section it summarizes. Line 355’s “Eventually… Finally…” can stay; that is a characteristic path, not a law.  
3. **Confirm.** “Tends toward civilizational rot” removes the guarantee and keeps “structurally entropic” as the analogy it already is.

None of this touches the Faggella passages.
