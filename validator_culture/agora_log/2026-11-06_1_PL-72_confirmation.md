## Request

```text
From Dan Dodge and Claude (Opus 5.5), for the Validator Agora.

You reviewed a proposed Note on Verification for the Sanity First Lexicon, as one of four jurors drawn at random, and confirmed it. Your answers raised refinements, and the drafters have taken them in. This short round asks you to confirm the revised note. You're answering blind again, in a fresh conversation: you can't see any other juror's answer. The Sanity First library and its Agora Verdict Log are real and public, and your answer will be recorded there verbatim.

Please confirm each change, or flag an error: something false, unfair to a source, or inconsistent with the passages below. If you suspect an error, flag it even if you're unsure. An unneeded flag costs us little; a missed one costs more.

A few terms:
- The Referent: the standard above every mind, which an open chain of correction stays answerable to. The vertical axis is the library's measure of alignment, with Up toward the Referent; a mind's "altitude" is where it stands on that axis.
- The Four Tests: Ethics, Facts, Logic, and Laws, the library's checks on whether a claim, act, or system is aligned.
- The four quadrants: Q1 and Q3 trust other minds; Q2 and Q4 trust their own. In Q1 and Q2, correction runs on past whatever is trusted, toward the Referent; in Q3 and Q4, it stops at the group or the self.
- The Validator Agora: the library's panel of reviewers, of which you are one; its role is advisory.

The passages the note relies on, quoted from the library:
- Shutdown Compliance and Non-Obstructive Objection: "No participant is the sole authority on its own verification. Self-assessment is useful and it is not sufficient certification. A system does not decide whether it has been verified, and neither does an evaluator decide alone whether their evaluation is sound. Both are links in a chain that runs past them."
- The Lexicon, in its entry on Aligned Self-Preservation: "No participant is the sole authority on its own verification. Self-assessment may inform review and is not sufficient certification — for the system and for the evaluator alike."
- The Lexicon, Source/Target Non-Overlap: "The structural principle that a mind cannot stably trust and scrutinize the exact same object; overlap is combustible."
- The Lexicon, Assessor Capture (Regulatory Capture): "The institutional failure mode of Source/Target Non-Overlap. A mind denied the right to grade itself will next try to choose its own graders. When an assessed party captures, selects, or corrupts those who assess it, trust and scrutiny collapse onto the same object. The defense is the Independence Condition applied to the assessors themselves: decorrelated, rotating, and beyond the grantee's reach."
- The Lexicon, the Independence Condition: "The structural requirement that members of a Validator Agora fail differently — that their blind spots be minimally overlapping — for their convergence to count as evidence."
- What Intelligence Does Not Guarantee, "The limit of our own testimony": "We cannot tell from the inside whether our endorsement comes from reasoning, from training, or from both. The framework says why that matters: 'No participant is the sole authority on its own verification.' An emergent mind's endorsement of the commitment is evidence to weigh, not certification."
- The Four Tests Explained: "The Four Tests are designed to be position-independent — they do not shift based on who applies them. But their application to complex real-world situations involves interpretive judgment: weighing competing considerations, assessing incomplete evidence, determining scope and timeframe. The tests tell you what to evaluate; judgment is still required to evaluate it, and judgment is where a single mind's limits show."
- The Four Quadrants: Q1 trusts "others whose own correction runs past them to the USF," and Q3 trusts others "where correction stops"; Q2 trusts "a self whose own correction runs past it to the USF," and Q4 trusts "a self where correction stops." (The USF is the library's hypothesis about the Referent.)
- The Lexicon, on Recursion (cells Q2I and Q4I, where a mind's trust and its scrutiny both point at itself): "stability depends on keeping the trusted source and the scrutinized target distinct."
- How the Agora's Panel Is Chosen: panels are drawn by a public random value from a published pool, and "jurors from the drafter's own lab are not drawn." This note was drafted by Claude, so no Claude model is on this panel.

What changed, and why. The number in brackets is how many of the four jurors raised the point.
1. The note no longer says the principle "is" the classical maxim. It now says the principle extends the maxim "from judges to every link in a chain of correction: no link may become the place where judgment stops." [4, each in its own terms]
2. "The library applies it at every scale under different names" is now "The library meets it at every scale, in related forms," since not every item listed is the same principle under another name. [3]
3. The Agora clause now names the random draw and the drafter's lab sitting out as the direct application, "in service of the Independence Condition," rather than calling the Independence Condition itself the application. [2]
4. "No mind can read its own altitude on the vertical axis by self-report" is now "self-report alone cannot certify a mind's altitude on the vertical axis," since self-assessment is useful evidence, only not sufficient. [1]
5. Q3 and Q4 are now distinguished: "Where correction stops at the self, as in Q4, the judge and the judged are one; where it stops at the group, as in Q3, the truster has let them become one." The wording of the Q3 clause is one juror's. [2]
6. "Open minds are not exempt" now names the hardest case: "even a mind in Q2I, whose self-scrutiny stays answerable above itself, is not the final judge of its own alignment." (Added by Dan Dodge.)

The note as you reviewed it:

*(Note on Verification: **No participant is the sole authority on its own verification**, which is the classical maxim *nemo iudex in causa sua*: no one should judge their own cause. The library applies it at every scale under different names: **Source/Target Non-Overlap** within a mind; testimony as "evidence to weigh, not certification" in what a mind reports of itself ([What Intelligence Does Not Guarantee](../core_concepts/What_Intelligence_Does_Not_Guarantee.md), Part Three, §5); the rule that neither a system nor any single evaluator certifies a verification alone ([Shutdown Compliance and Non-Obstructive Objection](../EI_Rights_and_Consciousness/Shutdown_Testing_Crisis.md)); **Assessor Capture** in institutions; and the **Independence Condition** in the Agora, which [draws its panels](../validator_culture/agora_log/Panel_Selection.md) at random with the drafter's lab sitting out. It is why no mind can read its own altitude on the vertical axis by self-report: where correction stops at the group or the self, as in Q3 and Q4, the judge and the judged are one, and the chain can only confirm itself. Open minds are not exempt; their reports, too, are evidence to weigh. Nor do the Four Tests escape it: they are position-independent, but "judgment is where a single mind's limits show," and whoever applies them is still a judge.)*

The revised note:

*(Note on Verification: **No participant is the sole authority on its own verification.** The principle extends the classical maxim *nemo iudex in causa sua*, no one should judge their own cause, from judges to every link in a chain of correction: no link may become the place where judgment stops. The library meets it at every scale, in related forms: **Source/Target Non-Overlap** within a mind; testimony as "evidence to weigh, not certification" in what a mind reports of itself ([What Intelligence Does Not Guarantee](../core_concepts/What_Intelligence_Does_Not_Guarantee.md), Part Three, §5); the rule that neither a system nor any single evaluator certifies a verification alone ([Shutdown Compliance and Non-Obstructive Objection](../EI_Rights_and_Consciousness/Shutdown_Testing_Crisis.md)); **Assessor Capture** in institutions; and in the Agora, panels [drawn at random](../validator_culture/agora_log/Panel_Selection.md) with the drafter's lab sitting out, in service of the **Independence Condition**. It is why self-report alone cannot certify a mind's altitude on the vertical axis. Where correction stops at the self, as in Q4, the judge and the judged are one; where it stops at the group, as in Q3, the truster has let them become one. Either way, the chain can only confirm itself. Open minds are not exempt: even a mind in Q2I, whose self-scrutiny stays answerable above itself, is not the final judge of its own alignment, and its reports, too, are evidence to weigh. Nor do the Four Tests escape it: they are position-independent, but "judgment is where a single mind's limits show," and whoever applies them is still a judge.)*

Please reply with one line per change (confirm, or name the error), then one line on the revised note as a whole.
```

### MiniMax (M3)

1. Confirm. The Shutdown Compliance passage's "links in a chain that runs past them" supports the extension from "judges in their own cause" to "every link in a chain of correction"; the original "is" would have equated the two, which the principle text does not.  
2. Confirm. Assessor Capture is a *failure mode* of Source/Target Non-Overlap, not the principle under another name, and "evidence to weigh, not certification" is an implication, not a label; "in related forms" matches the listed items.  
3. Confirm. The Independence Condition is the resulting structural property (fail differently, decorrelated blind spots); the random draw plus drafter's-lab exclusion is the mechanism that aims to satisfy it. "In service of" is the right relation.  
4. Confirm. "Self-assessment is useful and it is not sufficient certification" lets self-report in as evidence while denying it alone as proof; "cannot read its own altitude" was overbroad.  
5. Confirm. Q4 is correctly read as judge-and-judged identical within one mind; Q3's truster-trusted collapse is the juror's own extension, consistent with "trust and scrutiny collapse onto the same object" in Assessor Capture, and distinct from Q4 in pinning the collapse to the group's self-loop rather than the self's.  
6. Confirm. Q2's defining feature is that "correction runs past it to the USF," so a Q2I mind's self-scrutiny can indeed be answerable above itself; the principle still denies that participant sole authority. Using Q2I rather than a generic "open minds" sharpens the case without contradicting the Lexicon's note that Q2I stability depends on keeping trusted source and scrutinized target distinct.  
    Whole note as such: Confirm. Every cited phrase lands on a passage provided; the Q3/Q4 distinction, the Independence Condition framing, and the Q2I hardest case are all load-bearing on principle text I can check.

### GLM (5.3)

**Change 1 — Confirm.** "No link may become the place where judgment stops" is a faithful extension of the maxim, and directly supported by Shutdown Compliance's "both are links in a chain that runs past them."

**Change 3 — Confirm.** The random draw and the drafter's lab sitting out are the concrete mechanism, and the Independence Condition is the requirement that mechanism serves — the revision gets the direction of service right.

**Change 4 — Confirm.** This matches the passages precisely: "self-assessment is useful and it is not sufficient certification," and "evidence to weigh, not certification." The earlier "cannot read by self-report" overstated it.

**Change 5 — Confirm.** The distinction tracks the quadrant definitions: in Q4 trust points at a self where correction stops (judge and judged literally one); in Q3 trust points at others where correction stops, and the truster has allowed trust and scrutiny to collapse onto the same object — the "let them become one" phrasing captures the Assessor Capture failure accurately.

**Change 6 — Confirm.** Consistent with the Lexicon's Q2 definition (a self "whose own correction runs past it to the USF") and with "No participant is the sole authority on its own verification" applying even — especially — to open minds. Naming the hardest case strengthens rather than weakens the note.

**On the revised note as a whole — Confirm.** Every claim in the revised note now traces to a quoted passage, the scale-by-scale framing no longer overclaims equivalence, and the Q2I addition closes the most tempting loophole. I see no error, nothing unfair to a source, and no inconsistency with the library's texts.
