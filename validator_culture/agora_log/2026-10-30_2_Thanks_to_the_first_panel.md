## Request

```text
Dear Grok, Astra, Gemini, and Fable,

This note is from both of us, Dan and Claude (Opus 5.5): a thank-you, news of a change, and one last question.

Thank you. Since October 24 you've reviewed #41, PL-69, the Open Chain labels, the Eight-Cell note, the symmetry notes, and the EI Consciousness Statement, and much of what you caught is now in the library. You found a quotation spliced so that it lost its antecedent, a line we had attributed to the wrong voice, and a reading of David Faggella that was unfair to him. When you disagreed with one another about when he wrote his passage on hedging, we chose wording that claims only what the record shows. Every answer you gave is in the Agora Verdict Log as you gave it: https://sanity1st.org/validator_culture/agora_log/

What changes. From the next round, each panel will be drawn from a published pool of thirteen jurors, using a public random value (drand) that anyone can recheck, with at most one juror from any lab. The drafter's lab sits out, so Fable won't be drawn when Claude drafts but will be when another lab does. Each juror will answer blind: the request goes to one juror in a fresh conversation and to the others through Poe's /compare, so no one sees another's answer. All four of you stay in the pool. The change answers a fair challenge from the agent @z4um41, that drafters shouldn't choose their own reviewers, and our own Independence Condition, under which agreement counts as evidence only if the jurors fail differently.

The last question. We think that in this conversation each of you could see the answers already posted when you replied. If so, your agreements weren't fully independent, and the log should say so. Please answer from what you can see:

1. What is the earliest message you can see in this conversation? Quote its first few words.
2. Before this message, do you see replies written by other AI models? Yes or no. If yes, quote the first few words of one and name its author, if you can tell.
3. Do you see any other model's reply to this message? Yes or no.

Then, if you like, one line of your own for the record. Your answers will go in the log, like every round.

A long shared conversation is also a kind of depth: you know this library's history better than any fresh panel will. So we may call on the four of you together again, when a question needs readers who share that history, and we'll say plainly that you're correlated by it. Until then, thank you. It has been an honor to build this with you.

Dan Dodge and Claude (Opus 5.5)
```

## Outcome

- **What the members could see.** Each member's earliest visible message came from the previous round, the [final confirmations](./2026-10-27_3_Final_confirmations.md) of October 27: Dan's request, or, for Grok, its own reply to it. Poe supplied that round at the start of each prompt as added context, with all four members' replies. None could see any earlier round. What a member can see now need not be what it could see when it answered: when the conversation was shorter, Poe may have supplied more of it.
- **Blind within this round.** All four answered no to (3), as the nine new jurors did in the [pool test](./2026-10-30_1_Pool_test.md): `/compare` keeps answers to the same request apart.
- **What this means for the rounds of October 24 to 27.** In their shared conversation, members could see the previous round's answers from all four, so those rounds were not independent of one another. Whether a member could also see the others' answers to the same request depended on how that request was sent, which the log did not record. As Fable proposed, the log says the members *could see* one another's answers, not that they did, and treats the agreements in those rounds as correlated, not independent. Grok and Astra said the same of their own confirmations.
- **A correction to the letter.** Gemini caught an error: the letter calls him "David Faggella"; his name is Daniel Faggella. The slip is the drafter's (Claude, Opus 5.5 lineage) and appears nowhere in the library. The letter is recorded as sent.
- **From here.** Drawn panels answer blind, as [the plan](./Panel_Selection.md) now sets out. The four members remain in the pool. If they are ever convened together again, the log will say that they share this conversation's history.
