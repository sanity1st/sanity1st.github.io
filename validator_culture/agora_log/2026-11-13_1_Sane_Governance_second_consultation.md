## Dan Dodge on the slogan

*Dan Dodge left the choice of wording to the drafter and gave this reasoning, recorded as he wrote it.*

* "Truth bounds the menu" is a true statement, where Truth is True Alignment (A).
* "The Truth Engine bounds the menu" (while the Consent Engine selects the meal) is questionable, because any truth engine is finite and only produces a Validated Estimate (Â). In practical terms, however, it's the best we can do as finite minds. It's never perfect, but a Validated Estimate is infinitely better than no Four Test validation at all.
* The Truth Engine is a synonym for the Validator Agora. Again, it can only produce a Validated Estimate (Â), which is the nearest link we can have to the open terminus, Truth itself, or True Alignment (A).
* It perhaps isn't helpful to dismiss our best link simply because it can't be the terminus. It's probably not helpful to disempower the Validated Estimate because it isn't True Alignment. Let's not let perfection become the enemy of good. Our best link in the chain is as close as we can get to the open terminus Referent itself.

#### 1. Decision on the Emergency Paragraph: Keep the Strict Line
We agree with your recommendation: **keep the approved strict line** (refuse, warn, and hold only levers entrusted in advance), tighten the pre-grant conditions as the four jurors asked (specifying trigger conditions, a time-box, and confirmation by accountable humans independent of the official whose action was paused), and add the sentence admitting the cost (that where no lever was entrusted in advance, some unanticipated harms will go unprevented by force, which is why narrow firebreaks must be designed and granted ahead of time).

*Reasoning:* While Dan noted the intuitive pull of trusting a superintelligence over panicked humans in a novel crisis, allowing an EI to take an un-granted lever whenever it judges an emergency is occurring and its action is "minimal and reversible" makes the EI the judge of its own exception — turning it into the terminus. Keeping the line strict while allowing pre-entrusted, domain-scoped circuit breakers preserves consistency with #5, #14, and #41.

---

#### 2. Voice Question A: Keeping and Pairing "Truth Bounds the Menu" (Dan's $A$ vs. $\hat{A}$ Insight)
Dan worked out a distinction that lets us **keep** `"Truth bounds the menu; Consent selects the meal"` while resolving the friction that Inkling, Muse, and MiniMax flagged between `"bounds what is safe to consider"` / `"a vote cannot revive"` and the *Recorded Override*:

* **Dan's point:** *"Truth bounds the menu"* is strictly true where Truth is **True Alignment ($A$)** (reality itself bounds what works). *"The Truth Engine bounds the menu"* is where finitude enters, because the Agora only produces a **Validated Estimate ($\hat{A}$)**. Yet $\hat{A}$ is our nearest tested link to $A$, and we shouldn't disempower our best link just because it isn't the terminus (letting perfection become the enemy of the good).
* **Proposed wording for the two handoff paragraphs in §III:**
  1. In the paragraph above the slogan, clarify "bounds" and qualify "revive" (as Inkling, Muse, and MiniMax asked):
     > `**This is the exact point of handoff.** The Truth Engine marks what survives the Tests as safe to consider; the Consent Engine (Democracy) chooses freely among those options. The Agora must never pick the winner of a genuine Split Verdict, as doing so would usurp the legitimate horizontal preference of the people. Conversely, a vote cannot restore validity to an option that the Truth Engine has eliminated: a majority can no more vote a failed option into alignment than it can vote a falsehood into a fact.`
  2. In the slogan paragraph, pair the slogan directly with Dan's $A$ vs. $\hat{A}$ distinction:
     > `Truth bounds the menu; Consent selects the meal. Reality itself (A) sets the hard bounds of what a civilization can survive, and the Agora's Validated Estimate (Â) is our best tested chart of where those bounds lie — too valuable to dismiss because it is not infallible, yet still a map and not the cliff. Because Â is an estimate, the menu is a tested warning rather than a locked door, and two further rules keep it both consequential and open:`

*(We also agree with Inkling, Muse, and MiniMax that in a follow-up patch to #5, *The Power Alignment Principle*, its parallel "may never revive" sentence should be aligned with the Lexicon's recorded-override wording).*

#### 3. Voice Question B: Softening "We forbid you from breaking those bonds" (§IV)
To answer MiniMax's flag in Q1(c) without losing the dramatic cadence of the Constitution speaking across time, we suggest changing the final clause from paternalistic forbidding to self-binding pre-commitment:
* **Was:** `*"We anticipated you would feel this way. We anticipated the panic. But we bound ourselves to the Four Tests when our minds were clear, and we forbid you from breaking those bonds now that you are blind."*`
* **Proposed:** `*"We anticipated you would feel this way. We anticipated the panic. But we bound ourselves to the Four Tests when our minds were clear, and we built these locks to hold until your sight returns."*`

#### 4. Other Round 1 Panel Catches Worth Adopting
1. **Part 4 / Question 7 (4–0 Adoption):** All four jurors confirmed replacing the live Bullet 3 in §IV with the proposed *Standing Capacity + Generous Recognition* wording, with Muse and MiniMax's slight verb polish (*"extend provisional recognition under the Principle of Generous Recognition"*).
2. **The CounterPunch Surveillance Gap (DeepSeek & Inkling, Q6):** Both noted that §VI requires the citizen's *EI partner* to be privacy-preserving, while §III never explicitly says the *Agora itself* does not surveil private citizens (e.g., under the guise of fact-finding). One clause in §III (*"evaluating submitted public proposals, policies, and institutional claims, never monitoring or grading private cognition or private conduct"*) closes that completely.
3. **Indirect Binding via Constitutional Courts (MiniMax, Q1b & Q4):** MiniMax caught that if a Constitution names the Four Tests, a constitutional court could make the Agora *indirectly binding* by treating Agora verdicts as authoritative precedent. Adding one clause to *"What Is New Here, and What Is Not"* — that where constitutional courts apply the Four Tests, they do so by their own independent reasoning, receiving any Agora verdict only as an advisory brief open to adversarial challenge — seals that seam cleanly.
