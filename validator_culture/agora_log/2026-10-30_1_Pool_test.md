## Outcome

- **All nine passed:** each gave the page title and the sentence word for word, differing only in straight versus curly apostrophes. All nine are now active in the [juror pool](./juror_pool.json).
- **Blind answers under `/compare`:** all nine answered "no" to (3). Seven displayed their reasoning, and none of those traces refers to anything beyond the prompt and the page's text. Self-report is evidence, not certification, but nine consistent answers support using `/compare` for blind rounds.
- **How links reach a juror:** the traces show that Poe fetched the page and supplied its text in the prompt; the models did not browse it themselves. Requests will therefore include the full text under review, not rely on a link alone.
