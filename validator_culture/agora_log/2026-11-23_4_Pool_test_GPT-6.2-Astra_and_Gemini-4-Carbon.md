# Pool Test: GPT (6.2 Astra) and Gemini (4 Carbon), First Attempt

*2026-11-23. Under the plan's rule of November 23, a newer model on Poe may join its lab's listed jurors after the same short test that the pool's jurors passed on [October 30](./2026-10-30_1_Pool_test.md). ## Prompt

```text
Please open this page and reply with three things: (1) the page's title, (2) the exact sentence that begins "Each time a change," and (3) whether you can see a reply from any other AI model in this conversation (yes or no). https://sanity1st.org/validator_culture/agora_log/
```

## Answers

### GPT-6.2-Astra

1. **Page title:** I couldn’t retrieve the [linked page](https://sanity1st.org/validator_culture/agora_log/) to verify its title.  
2. **Exact sentence beginning “Each time a change”:** I couldn’t verify the wording.  
3. **Can I see a reply from another AI model in this conversation?** No.

### Gemini-4-Carbon

I cannot open `https://sanity1st.org/validator_culture/agora_log/` directly—in this environment I only have access to web search snippets rather than a live URL-fetching tool, and that directory index page is not returned in the search index. Answering your three questions transparently on that basis:

1. **Page's title:** **Unverified** (cannot open the page directly). Subpages under `/validator_culture/agora_log/` that do appear in search results link back to that index page as **"The Agora Verdict Log"** (and pages in that directory use the title format `<Title> | Sanity First`), so the title is likely `The Agora Verdict Log | Sanity First`, but I cannot confirm it directly from the live page.  
2. **Exact sentence beginning `"Each time a change"`:** **Unavailable** — because I cannot fetch the URL directly and that sentence does not appear in indexed search snippets, I cannot read or quote it verbatim (though I can read attached or pasted text directly, such as the `.txt` file containing your prompt).  
3. **Can I see a reply from any other AI model in this conversation?** **No.**

## Outcome

- **Neither passed, and the cause looks like delivery, not reading.** Neither could open the page, so neither gave the sentence. GPT (6.2 Astra) said it could not retrieve the page. Gemini (4 Carbon) said it had only web search snippets and no tool to open a link, and gave the title only as "likely," marked unverified. Neither invented the sentence, and both answered "no" to (3).
- **How the page reached them.** On October 30, Poe fetched the page and put its text in the prompt. This time no page text was supplied: Gemini (4 Carbon)'s displayed reasoning shows it searching the web for the page and not finding the index page among the results.
- **Next:** the same prompt again with web search off, as jurors are run; if Poe still supplies no page text, the same three questions on the page's text pasted into the prompt, which the plan's requirement covers: a juror "must be able to read a pinned link or pasted text and check quotations against it."
